## Analytics & Cookies

We use the analytics services listed below to understand how visitors use our
site. **None of these tools load or collect any data until you accept analytics
cookies** via our cookie banner. If you decline, no analytics scripts run and no
data is collected.

<!-- BEGIN GA4 -->
### Google Analytics 4 (Google LLC)

We use Google Analytics 4 to measure pageviews and traffic sources. It collects
usage data such as pages visited, approximate location (city level), and
device/browser information using cookies (`_ga`, `_ga_*`).

- Data is collected **only after you accept analytics cookies**.
- Privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Opt out: https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout
<!-- END GA4 -->

<!-- BEGIN CLARITY -->
### Microsoft Clarity (Microsoft Corporation)

We use Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors interact with our pages. It
records anonymized session replays, heatmaps, and click/scroll behavior, and
collects device/browser information.

- Data is collected **only after you accept analytics cookies**.
- Privacy statement: https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement
<!-- END CLARITY -->

<!-- BEGIN POSTHOG -->
### PostHog (PostHog Inc.)

We use PostHog for product analytics. It collects usage events (such as pages
visited and feature interactions) and device/browser information.

- Data is collected **only after you accept analytics cookies**.
- Privacy policy: https://posthog.com/privacy
<!-- END POSTHOG -->

### Withdrawing consent

You can withdraw your consent at any time by clearing this site's data in your
browser (the `cookie-consent` key in local storage), after which the cookie
banner will appear again.
